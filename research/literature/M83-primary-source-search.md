## Scope

- Search and inspection date: 2026-07-31.
- Purpose: compare the representative M82 claims with primary literature
  identified by the external Korean feedback.
- This is a bounded related-work audit, not a complete priority survey.
- A missing exact-phrase or theorem match is **not** evidence that a project
  result is novel, first, or independently discovered.
- A missing match is not evidence that a project result is novel.

## Search routes

The audit used publisher/DOI records, author-hosted bibliographies and
manuscripts, academy repositories, and exact-phrase web searches. Search
families included:

- Pollard \(p-1\), hereditary promises, fresh random bases, recursive complete
  factorization, and explicit success bounds;
- Williams \(p+1\), Lucas root counts, discriminant degeneracy, and success
  probability;
- finite separating systems and binary membership signatures;
- vanishing sums of roots of unity, rational cosine relations, common-step
  relations, and orders four and six;
- multi-exponent addition chains and multiplication schedules; and
- product trees, remainder trees, batch GCD, smooth parts, and separator
  coverage; and
- odd-cycle transversal, iterative compression, three-way compression
  partitions, and vertex-separator reductions.

Exact-phrase searches for the repository's combined formulations did not
produce a direct match for the hereditary Pollard theorem, the exact
common-step/\(\Phi_4\)/\(\Phi_6\) observable, or the selector-specific
balanced-semiprime span barriers. These negative search results are recorded
only to delimit what was inspected. They do not discharge a novelty or
priority burden.
