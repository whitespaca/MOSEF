## Inspected results and methods

Pages 229--234 translate trigonometric Diophantine equations to linear
relations among roots of unity and use field automorphisms to split a
vanishing sum into equivalence classes. Theorem 2 gives an exact
square-free-order vanishing criterion, and Theorem 3 gives a parametric
description of the resulting solutions.

Pages 234--238 develop a theory of short vanishing sums. Theorem 6 classifies
nonempty vanishing sums of length at most nine, up to the paper's
identifications. Theorem 7 derives a finite list of rational linear
relations among at most four cosines of rational angles. Pages 239--240 apply
the same Galois/root-of-unity viewpoint to geometric examples and identify
the low orders \(1,2,3,4,6\) in one triangle argument.
