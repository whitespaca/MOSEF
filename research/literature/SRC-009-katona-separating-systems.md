## Inspected mechanism

Katona calls a family \(\mathcal A=\{A_1,\ldots,A_m\}\) of subsets of a
finite set \(H\) a separating system when every two distinct elements of
\(H\) have different membership in at least one \(A_i\). The introduction
notes the unconstrained binary-information bound and studies the minimum
number of sets when each \(A_i\) has bounded cardinality. Page 175 attributes
the separating-system concept to earlier information-theoretic work of
Rényi.

This establishes that pairwise distinction by binary membership signatures
is classical combinatorial background.
