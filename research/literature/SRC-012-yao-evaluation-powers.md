## Inspected abstract-level result

to compute a prescribed set
\(\{x^{n_1},\ldots,x^{n_p}\}\), generalizing Brauer's addition-chain result.
The official keywords are "addition chains" and "Brauer's theorem."

This establishes only broad historical context: simultaneous evaluation of
